# Add the demo-day thesis and fix what it exposed

**Files changed:**
- `backend/scripts/make_demo_thesis.py` (new), `samples/demo-thesis.pdf` (new), `samples/README.md`
- `backend/app/parse.py` (merge consecutive lines into paragraphs)
- `backend/app/scrub.py` (collapse repeated spaces, trim names at role words, skip organisations, second pass that replaces a found name everywhere)
- `backend/app/main.py`, `frontend/app.js` (Open sample document now loads the demo thesis)

Added a 12-page fictional thesis for demo day with planted names, emails, a phone number, a BU ID, a discussion section, six participants taken from real rows of the organizers' data (four high, one medium, one low risk) and four format problems. Building it exposed real bugs: PDFs that store each justified line as its own block split sentences at every line end, so names and participant details were missed; parse.py now merges closely spaced, aligned lines into paragraphs. Justified text also had double spaces that broke name matching, an all-caps author name on the title page was not detected, "Elena Marchetti Second Reader" was taken as one name, and "Mugar Memorial Library" as a person. Scrub now collapses spaces, cuts names at role words, ignores organisation phrases, and replaces every occurrence of a name found anywhere in the document, ignoring case. The ECE lab report, the rubric and the test thesis give the same results as before. Checked the whole flow in the browser with the demo thesis.

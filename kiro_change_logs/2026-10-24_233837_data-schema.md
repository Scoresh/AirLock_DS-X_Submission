# Profile the NSSE data and document column roles

**Files changed:**
- `backend/scripts/profile_data.py` (new), `docs/DATA_SCHEMA.md` (new)

Added an aggregates-only profiler for the organizers' NSSE synthetic data. It writes each column's type, distinct count, null share and a proposed privacy role (1 direct identifier, 20 quasi-identifiers, 110 sensitive survey attributes, 1 other), and value counts only for quasi-identifiers with fewer than 30 values, with counts below 5 suppressed. No rows are printed or written.
